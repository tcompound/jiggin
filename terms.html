This Privacy Policy explains what information VouchRestore collects when you use the Service, why we collect it, and the choices available to you.

01Information we collect

To operate the vouch ledger and its commands, we collect and store:

  • Discord identifiers — user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, and role IDs needed to attribute and display vouches correctly.
  • Vouch content — the text of a vouch, the member it was given to, who gave it, and its timestamp.
  • Configuration data — server settings such as the vouch channel, required vouch format, and cooldown periods.
  • Command usage logs — which commands were run, by whom, and when, used for the audit log and abuse prevention.

We do not collect message content outside of what is submitted directly to VouchRestore's commands, and we do not access direct messages.

02How we use information

Collected data is used to:

  • Maintain the persistent vouch ledger and power the restore, leaderboard, and audit log commands.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, duplicate vouching, and abuse of the Service.
  • Provide support when a server administrator contacts us with an issue.
  • Maintain and improve the reliability of the Service.

03Data storage and retention

Vouch and configuration data is retained for as long as VouchRestore remains in a server, so that the ledger can be restored if the server or its channels are altered. If VouchRestore is removed from a server, associated data may be retained for a limited period to allow re-adding the bot without losing the ledger, after which it is deleted on a routine schedule.

04Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. Information is shared only with:

  • Discord, as necessary for the Service to operate through Discord's API.
  • Infrastructure and hosting providers that store data on our behalf, under obligations to protect it.
  • Third parties when required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of VouchRestore, its users, or others.
